# About Pacific Light Shades & Blinds

## Welcome to Pacific Light Shades & Blinds

At Pacific Light Shades & Blinds, we believe that beautiful, functional window treatments shouldn't break the bank. We're a direct-to-consumer custom window shades and blinds company dedicated to bringing you premium quality at unbeatable prices—typically **50% less than major retailers** like Home Depot.

## Our Mission

We're on a mission to make high-quality, custom window treatments accessible to everyone. By working directly with factories and eliminating the middleman, we pass significant savings directly to you without compromising on quality, style, or service.

## What Makes Us Different

### **Factory-Direct Pricing**
We ship directly from the factory, cutting out brand premiums, dealer markups, and showroom overhead. This means you get the same premium materials and craftsmanship at a fraction of the cost.

### **Fully Customizable Solutions**
Every window is unique, and so should be your shades. We offer **complete customization**—from precise measurements to fabric selection, opacity levels, and control options. Choose from over **1,000 sophisticated fabrics** in multiple opacity levels, with child-safe cordless or motorized controls.

### **Two Service Options to Fit Your Needs**

**Full Service:** Our recommended option for a hands-off, worry-free experience. Our team handles everything—from professional in-home measurements and design recommendations to expert installation and ongoing maintenance. Perfect for busy homeowners who want it done right the first time.

**DIY Installation:** Ideal for confident DIYers who want to save even more. We provide detailed measuring instructions, video tutorials, and all necessary hardware. Install at your own pace with our easy-to-follow guides.

### **Expert Design Consultation**
Our experienced interior designers work with you to understand your style, budget, and needs. After reviewing your room photos and requirements, we recommend several shade options that perfectly match your vibe and budget.

### **Smart Home Integration**
Our motorized shades integrate seamlessly with today's leading smart home ecosystems through Matter and the Connector platform. Control your shades through apps or voice assistants with simple setup and reliable performance.

### **Professional Installation & Support**
When you choose our Full Service option, skilled professionals ensure your window treatments are installed to the highest standards. Every installation is handled with meticulous attention to detail, so your shades look great and function smoothly.

### **Comprehensive Warranty**
We stand behind our products with a comprehensive warranty—**2 years for shades and 5 years for motors**. Plus, we offer free maintenance within the warranty period, so you can enjoy long-lasting performance with complete confidence.
